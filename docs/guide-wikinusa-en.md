# WIKINUSA APPLICATION GUIDE

## Intro

Looking to explore knowledge, dictionaries, and literature in indigenous and regional languages? With the WikiNusa app, you can seamlessly read Wikipedia articles, search word definitions on Wiktionary, and access open textbooks and folklore on Wikibooks across Indonesian and Pacific regional languages.

## WIKIMEDIA LOGIN

1. Open the Side Drawer Menu. The login button is located at the bottom.
![Side drawer menu](images/laci-menu-samping.webp)

2. Tap LOGIN. The official Wikimedia authentication page will load.
![Wikimedia login screen](images/halaman-login.webp)

3. Enter your Wikimedia username and password, then tap LOG IN.

4. If you have 2FA enabled, enter your two-factor verification code.

5. Authorize access for the WikiNusa app.
![Authorizing Wikimedia access](images/otentikasi-wikimedia.webp)

6. Tap Allow. You are now ready to edit articles directly inside the app.

**Note:** Logging in is optional; you can browse and read all content without an account.

## WIKINUSA FEATURES

### How to start a new article or entry?
- Tap the **START (BÖRÖGÖ)** button at the end of the Home Screen.
- Or open the Side Drawer Menu and select **Create New Page** in Quick Shortcuts.

The form automatically adapts to Wikipedia, Wiktionary, or Wikibooks.

*(Note: [Check the Nusa Keyboard Guide](guide-keyboard-en.md) to type special regional characters like ö and ŵ).*

### How to edit an existing article?
While viewing an article, tap the **Pencil Icon** in the Floating Action Bar.
![Article screen](images/halaman-artikel.webp)

### How to search for articles or words?
Type your keywords into the top search field on the Home Screen. Autocomplete suggestions will appear immediately.
![Search results](images/hd-mencari.webp)

### How to bookmark favorite articles?
Tap the **Bookmark Icon** on the Floating Action Bar while viewing an article.

## WIKI MARKUP & EDITING

### Bold text
Wrap words in three single quotes (`'''`), e.g. `'''bold text'''`.

### Italic text
Wrap words in two single quotes (`''`), e.g. `''italic text''`.

### Headings
Wrap headings with equal signs, e.g. `== Heading 2 ==`.

### Internal wikilinks
Wrap target article titles in double square brackets (`[[...]]`), e.g. `[[Nias]]` or `[[Target Page|Anchor Text]]`.

## Wikipedia
Wikipedia is an encyclopedia providing long-form, comprehensive coverage of concepts, historical events, biographies, and cultural topics.

## Wiktionary
Wiktionary is a linguistic dictionary focusing on word definitions, derivations, phonetic IPA pronunciations, and dialect tags.

## Wikibooks
Wikibooks hosts books, oral poetry, traditional chants, folklore, educational textbooks, essays, and stories.

## WikiChat (Community Discussions)
WikiChat connects directly to Wikimedia talk pages (such as village pumps and user discussions).
1. Open the Side Drawer Menu and select **Chat / Obrolan** under Modules.
2. Tap **+** to start a new topic thread, enter the subject and message, and send (with CAPTCHA support).
3. Tap **Reply** on any message to post a threaded reply or quote snippets.

## Community Newsletter
Browse community periodicals (such as the *Turia* newsletter on Nias Wikipedia).
1. Open the Side Drawer Menu and tap **Newsletter**.
2. Select any current or archived edition.
3. Use the Table of Contents (TOC) to navigate directly to sections of interest.

## Interactive Crosswords
Educational crossword puzzles dynamically generated from regional Wiktionary vocabulary.
1. Open the Side Drawer Menu and tap **Crosswords**.
2. Choose daily, monthly, or yearly puzzle collections.
3. Tap grid cells to enter letters and view Across/Down clues.
4. Use **Hint** for letter verification.
5. Add the native Android Crossword widget to your home screen.

## Language Course
Structured interactive language lessons (such as the *Sulu* series on Wiktionary and Wikibooks).
1. Open the Side Drawer Menu and tap **Language Course**.
2. Work through thematic dialogue lessons.
3. Listen to native-speaker audio pronunciations.
4. Complete interactive vocabulary and grammar quizzes.

## Cultural Gallery & Commons Media
High-resolution cultural heritage and architectural photography curated from Wikimedia Commons.
1. Open the Side Drawer Menu and tap **Gallery**.
2. Swipe through photo carousels of heritage sites, traditional houses (*omo hada*), and cultural artifacts.
3. Tap any photo to open the fullscreen zoom viewer with pinch-to-zoom support.
