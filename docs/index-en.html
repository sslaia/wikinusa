Interactive & Community Modules

WikiNusa goes beyond article reading by introducing 5 powerful modules designed for learning, community discussions, and cultural celebration.

💬

WikiChat

Community Discussions & Talk Pages

Real-time talk page discussion forum directly integrated with Wikimedia talk namespaces (such as Village pump or Teahouse). Chat, coordinate projects, and debate articles in a modern thread interface.

  • Direct live integration with Wikipedia talk pages
  • Threaded replies and nested discussion hierarchy
  • Reply quoting, live preview, and CAPTCHA support
  • Unread badge notification system for active conversations
📰

Community Newsletter

Bulletins & News

A portal for community bulletins sourced directly from the Wikipedia's designate page. Stay updated with workshop schedules, contest announcements, and grassroots milestones.

  • Browse latest publications alongside historical archives
  • Structured table of contents (TOC) navigation per edition
  • Clean, distraction-free reading experience for news articles
🧩

Interactive Crosswords

Wiktionary Vocabulary Puzzles

Educational crossword puzzles that dynamically source authentic vocabulary and definitions from local Wiktionaries. Sharpen language mastery through engaging gameplay for both learners and fluent speakers.

  • Interactive touch grid with intuitive across and down clues
  • Clues linked directly to official Wiktionary definitions
  • Letter-by-letter validation, hint revealing, and sound effects
🎓

Language Course

Interactive Learning

Accessible regional language learning modules on Wiktionary. They help beginners and the younger generation learn practical conversation, grammatical structures, and local idioms.

  • Content directly from the relevant page on the Wiktionary.
🖼️

Photo Gallery

Commons High-Res Photography

A curated visual celebration of cultural heritage, traditional vernacular architecture, customary attire, megalithic monuments, and scenic landscapes sourced from Wikimedia Commons.

  • High-resolution photo carousels with smooth swipe gestures
  • Rich metadata details, photographer credits, and licensing info
  • Full-screen image viewer with pinch-to-zoom support
